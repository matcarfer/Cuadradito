# Privacy

[Español](PRIVACY.es.md) · **English**

Cuadradito **does not collect, send or share any data**.

- **No internet permission**, no ads, no analytics, no third-party libraries.
- **Bluetooth** (`BLUETOOTH_SCAN` with *neverForLocation*, `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT`; on Android 6–11 the
  system requires location permission to scan): only to find and talk to the square.
- **Microphone** (`RECORD_AUDIO`, asked only when you tap *Ecualizador*): only while the equalizer is
  on screen, to draw the bars. Sound is not recorded, not stored and never leaves the phone.
- **What it stores, only on the phone:** the address of the last square, your settings (brightness,
  speed, colour fix, last text) and *My animations*. Uninstalling deletes everything.
- **Images you import** are reduced to 16×16 on the phone; the original is not copied or sent.
- **Nothing runs in the background.**
